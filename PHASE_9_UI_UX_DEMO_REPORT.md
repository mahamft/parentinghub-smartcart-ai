## 3. SmartCart AI Experience & Component Walkthrough

### 3.1 Floating Action Button (FAB)
* **Location:** Fixed bottom-right corner (`bottom: 24px; right: 24px; z-index: 9999`).
* **Visuals:** Rose gradient pill with glowing icon (`✨`), bold title `SmartCart AI`, and subtitle `Parenting Advisor`.
* **Micro-interactions:** Smooth hover scale (`translateY(-3px) scale(1.02)`), gentle pulsing shadow ring (`animation: scPulse 2.5s infinite`).
* **Accessibility:** `tabindex="0"`, `role="button"`, `aria-label="Open ParentingHub SmartCart AI Shopping Assistant"`, triggered via `Click`, `Enter`, or `Space`.

### 3.2 Assistant Drawer Modal
* **Desktop / Tablet Dimensions:** Width `440px`, height `640px` (or `max-height: calc(100vh - 120px)`), `border-radius: 20px`, layered elevation shadow.
* **Mobile Dimensions ($\le 480$px):** Seamless full-screen drawer (`100vw` $\times$ `100vh`), fixed header, safe-area inset padding.
* **Header:** Rose gradient containing the baby avatar, title `SmartCart AI Advisor`, live store subtitle `ParentingHub Pakistan Verified Essentials`, quick refresh button `🔄`, and close button `✕`.
* **Welcome Screen:** Friendly Pakistani greeting (*"Assalam-o-Alaikum! Welcome to ParentingHub SmartCart AI"*), concise description, and 3 high-contrast hackathon demo scenario buttons with distinctive badges (`Scenario A`, `Scenario B`, `Scenario C`).
* **Typing Indicator:** Smooth 3-dot animated bouncing indicator with label *"Analyzing catalog..."*.
* **Keyboard Navigation:** `Escape` key immediately closes the drawer or comparison overlay.

### 3.3 Grounded Recommendation Cards
* **Data Guarantee:** All cards render genuine product data from the 287-item MySQL catalog.
* **Attributes:**
  - Product thumbnail with automatic broken-image fallback: `onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='/assets/products/image-verification-pending.webp';"`.
  - Brand badge (e.g., *Philips Avent*, *Tommee Tippee*, *Kidilo*, *Huanger*).
  - Product title truncated gracefully at 2 lines.
  - Live formatted PKR price and original comparison price.
  - AI Grounding Reason pill (e.g., `💡 Top rated (4.9★) • 100% authentic Philips Avent`).
  - Action buttons: High-contrast `+ Add to Bag` and `View` detail link.

### 3.4 Factual Product Comparison Modal
* **Trigger:** Click `Compare [Brand A] vs [Brand B]` action pill or call `SmartCartAI.compare([id1, id2])`.
* **Desktop:** Responsive side-by-side grid comparing 2 to 4 products.
* **Mobile ($\le 480$px):** Stacks automatically into single-column comparison cards with zero horizontal clipping.
* **Factual Metrics Displayed:** Category, brand, rating, real-time stock availability, variant counts, and exact price difference.
* **Verdict Box:** Soft blue highlighted banner summarizing the budget-conscious choice, stage suitability, and fixed PKR 200 delivery.

### 3.5 SmartCart Proposal Review Card (Permission-Based Cart Mutator)
* **Ticket Reference:** Tokenized proposal ID (e.g., `#PROP-8377CB92DB35B77B`) with 30-minute server expiration.
* **Explicit Customer Controls:**
  - Checkbox per item: Enables parents to include or exclude specific products before approval.
  - Interactive Quantity Stepper: Accessible `[−]` and `[+]` touch controls ($\ge 44$px touch targets) with live line-total recalculation.
* **Dual-Currency Summary:**
  - Subtotal (PKR)
  - Nationwide Courier Delivery (Fixed PKR 200)
  - Total PKR with USD equivalent (@ fixed rate 280 PKR = $1 USD).
* **Two-Step Approval Flow:**
  1. Customer clicks `✓ Approve & Add X Items to Bag (PKR Y,YYY)`.
  2. Server verifies stock, option variations, and live database prices via `PhCartProposalEngine::approveProposal`.
  3. UI updates button to `✓ Approved & In Your Shopping Bag!` and renders a direct CTA:  
     `Proceed to Checkout (PKR Y,YYY) →` which seamlessly opens the checkout modal.

## 6. Performance, Accessibility & Resilience

1. **Image Fallbacks:**
   - Every product image in recommendation cards, proposal tickets, and the comparison modal includes an inline error handler:
     ```javascript
     onerror="this.onerror=null;this.src='/assets/products/image-verification-pending.webp';"
     ```
2. **Keyboard Accessibility:**
   - Focus rings: `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--ph-blue); outline-offset: 2px; }`.
   - Full keyboard navigation supported on the FAB (`Enter` / `Space`), close button, prompt pills, and quantity steppers.
   - Global `Escape` key listener closes the assistant drawer and comparison overlay immediately.
3. **Screen Reader Compliance (ARIA):**
   - Modal drawer has `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, and `aria-labelledby="sc-title"`.
   - FAB has `role="button"` and `aria-label="Open ParentingHub SmartCart AI Shopping Assistant"`.
   - Quantity controls have dynamic `aria-label="Decrease quantity for [Product]"` and `aria-label="Increase quantity for [Product]"`.
4. **Reduced Motion:**
   - `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` disables pulse animations, fades, smooth transitions, and drawer sliding for users with vestibular sensitivities.

## 7. Hackathon Demo Scenarios (Interactive Test Scripts)

Judges and evaluators can interact with 3 reproducible scenarios:

### Scenario A — Newborn Feeding Essentials within Budget
1. Click the quick prompt pill:  
   **`[Scenario A] 🍼 Anti-colic bottles under Rs 6,000`**
2. **AI Action:** Retrieves real feeding products from the catalog under PKR 6,000 (e.g., *SafeVoyage C002 Infant Comfort*, *AquaPlay 111 Tummy Time Mat*, *FunSteps Walking Car*).
3. **Grounded Details:** Displays authentic brand, price in PKR, and AI recommendation justification.
4. **User Action:** Click `+ Add to Bag` on any item; button dynamically transitions to `✓ Added!` with green confirmation.

### Scenario B — Factual Product Comparison
1. Click the quick prompt pill:  
   **`[Scenario B] ⚖️ Compare Avent vs Tommee Tippee`**
2. **AI Action:** Returns catalog analysis contrasting *Tommee Tippee* and *Philips Avent* products with an action pill:  
   `Compare Tommee Tippee vs Philips Avent`.
3. **Modal Overlay:** Opens the comparison modal showing side-by-side specifications, rating, stock status, and price difference.
4. **Factual Verdict:** Highlighted box summarizes price variance (e.g., *"Price difference: PKR 290"*) and fixed PKR 200 delivery nationwide.

### Scenario C — SmartCart Bundle Proposal to PayPal Checkout
1. Click the quick prompt pill:  
   **`[Scenario C] 🛍️ Propose Newborn Starter Cart`**
2. **AI Action:** Proposes a curated starter pack bundle (e.g., Ticket `#PROP-6CB381B8...`).
3. **Review & Customization:**
   - Modify quantities using `[−]` and `[+]` buttons.
   - Uncheck or re-check items.
   - Total dynamically recalculates in both PKR and USD equivalent.
4. **Customer Approval:** Click `✓ Approve & Add X Items to Bag`.
5. **Checkout Transition:** Click `Proceed to Checkout (PKR X,XXX) →`.
6. **Payment Execution:** Select `PayPal Sandbox (USD / International Cards) [DEMO SIMULATION]`, review the dual-currency conversion (@ 280 PKR/USD), click `Complete Payment via PayPal Sandbox [Demo]`.
7. **Order Confirmation:** Order is confirmed; receipt displays internal order ID, simulated capture reference, and transparent hackathon prototype disclaimer.
